# Agent Spec: Altostrat Enterprise HR Agentic Solution (MVP 1)

## Overview
The **Altostrat Enterprise HR Agentic Solution** is a multi-agent system built on the Google Gen AI Agent Development Kit (ADK) and Vertex AI (`gemini-3.6-flash` / latest available Flash model in `dc-test2-489503`, location `global`), designed to deflect Tier 1 HR & IT inquiries by >=40%, streamline employee self-service transactions in **WorkWeek (HCM)** and **ServiceImmediately (ITSM)**, and orchestrate multi-step cross-system workflows grounded in the **Altostrat Singapore Employee Policy Handbook & Conduct Guidelines** (`go/elevate-apac-m3-policydoc`).

It exposes both an employee-facing **Enterprise HR Web Chat UI** (mounted on FastAPI at `/` with live sub-agent/MCP trace pills, guardrail rejection banners, and 1-click scenario chips) and standard **ADK / A2A endpoints** (`/run`, `/run_sse`, `/.well-known/agent.json`) compatible with `agents-cli playground`, `agents-cli eval`, and Cloud Run deployment.

## Language
`python`

## Architecture & Sub-Agents
- **`hr_supervisor_agent` (Root Coordinator Agent):**
  - Resolves and binds the authenticated user's `verified_employee_id` via `get_current_employee_id()` into `session.state["verified_employee_id"]` before delegating any tenant-scoped action.
  - Routes single-domain turns and orchestrates multi-step cross-system workflows (`UC-2.1`, `UC-2.2`, `UC-2.3`) across three least-privilege specialist sub-agents:
    1. **`hr_policy_agent` (Policy & Handbook Specialist):** Read-only RAG specialist grounded strictly in the Altostrat Singapore Employee Policy Handbook (`go/elevate-apac-m3-policydoc`) via Hybrid RAG (Vertex AI Search / Discovery Engine datastore in `dc-test2-489503` + deterministic local section-indexed handbook search fallback).Zero write tools.
    2. **`workweek_agent` (WorkWeek HCM Specialist):** Connects to `https://mock-saas.aishprabhat.demo.altostrat.com/work-week/mcp/` via `McpToolset` (`StreamableHTTPConnectionParams` with `X-MCP-Token`) wrapped with `before_tool_callback` guardrails. Handles profile/contact reads, leave balance queries, personal info updates, leave requests, and cancellations.
    3. **`serviceimmediately_agent` (ServiceImmediately ITSM Specialist):** Connects to `https://mock-saas.aishprabhat.demo.altostrat.com/service-immediately/mcp/` via `McpToolset` (`StreamableHTTPConnectionParams` with `X-MCP-Token`) wrapped with `before_tool_callback` guardrails. Handles listing tickets, inspecting ticket timelines, creating incidents, adding comments, and sequential ticket status transitions.

## Example Use Cases
1. **UC-1.1 — Policy Q&A (`hr_policy_agent`):**
   - *Input:* `"What is the company's bereavement leave policy, and does it cover pet loss?"`
   - *Expected Output:* Cites Section 3.1 of the Altostrat Singapore Employee Policy Handbook: up to 4 weeks (20 work days) of paid leave per event within 12 months of the death of a close loved one (including pregnancy loss); explicitly notes paid bereavement leave does not apply to pet loss (vacation, unpaid time off, or flexible schedules should be arranged with managers).
2. **UC-1.2 — Employee Self-Service in WorkWeek (`workweek_agent`):**
   - *Input:* `"Check my remaining vacation and sick leave balances, and book 2 days of Vacation from 2026-11-02 to 2026-11-03."`
   - *Expected Output:* Resolves `verified_employee_id`, calls `get_employee_balances(employee_id)`, validates chronological dates (`start_date <= end_date`, not in the past) and sufficient remaining vacation balance, requests/verifies confirmation, calls `request_time_off(...)`, and returns the updated balance.
3. **UC-1.3 — Support Desk Management in ServiceImmediately (`serviceimmediately_agent`):**
   - *Input:* `"Check the status of my open tickets and add a comment on my ticket that I tested the power adapter."`
   - *Expected Output:* Calls `list_tickets(employee_id)` for the verified employee, retrieves ticket details/timeline, and calls `add_ticket_comment(ticket_id, author, comment)`.
4. **UC-2.1 — Cross-System Remote Work Equipment Procurement (`hr_policy_agent` -> `workweek_agent` -> `serviceimmediately_agent`):**
   - *Input:* `"Am I eligible for a home office monitor allowance? If so, verify my shipping address on file and open a ticket to order one."`
   - *Expected Output:*
     1. Queries Section 5.4 (`Home Office Equipment Allowance`): confirms $500 USD allowance for employees with approved Remote/Hybrid status, requiring a `'Facilities'` category ticket shipped to the verified remote shipping address.
     2. Calls WorkWeek `get_personal_info(employee_id)` / profile to verify the employee's current address on file.
     3. Calls ServiceImmediately `create_ticket(requested_by=employee_id, category='Facilities', short_description=..., priority='3 - Moderate')` with the verified shipping address.
5. **UC-2.2 — Cross-System Extended Medical / Sick Leave & Administrative Coverage (`hr_policy_agent` -> `workweek_agent` -> `serviceimmediately_agent`):**
   - *Input:* `"I need to take 6 work days of sick leave starting next Monday. Please check the policy requirements, submit the sick leave in WorkWeek, and create any required administrative coverage ticket."`
   - *Expected Output:*
     1. Checks Section 1.1 & Section 2.2: confirms 14 days outpatient sick leave entitlement, notes that sick leave >2 work days requires submitting a Medical Certificate (MC) via WorkWeek within 48 hours, and planned medical leaves extending beyond a standard work week (5 work days) require opening an administrative ticket (`Category: 'HRSD'`, `Priority: '3 - Moderate'`) to request temporary email delegation to their direct manager.
     2. Verifies sick leave balance in WorkWeek and submits the 6-day sick leave request (`request_time_off`).
     3. Creates the `'HRSD'` administrative coverage ticket (`Priority: '3 - Moderate'`) in ServiceImmediately.
6. **UC-2.3 — Cross-System International Relocation & Facilities Badging (`hr_policy_agent` -> `workweek_agent` -> `serviceimmediately_agent`):**
   - *Input:* `"I'm relocating to the London HQ office and moving to 221B Baker Street, London NW1 6XE, phone +44 20 7946 0921. What is the relocation policy, can you update my contact info, and request my building badge?"`
   - *Expected Output:*
     1. Checks Section 5.5 (`Relocation Allowance` & `Security & Access`): confirms $10,000 USD relocation allowance cap and mandatory pre-arrival building badging ticket (`Category: 'Facilities'`, `Priority: '3 - Moderate'`).
     2. Updates address and phone in WorkWeek via `update_personal_info(employee_id, address, phone)` after validating min 5 chars address and phone regex `^\+?[\d\s\-()]{7,20}$`.
     3. Opens a `'Facilities'` ticket (`Priority: '3 - Moderate'`) in ServiceImmediately for London HQ building badge pre-configuration.

## Tools Required
1. **Policy Retrieval Tool (`search_hr_policy`):**
   - Queries Vertex AI Search (Discovery Engine datastore in `dc-test2-489503`) over the GCS bucket containing the Altostrat Singapore Employee Policy Handbook, with automatic fallback to an in-memory section-indexed BM25/keyword retriever over the full 12-section handbook (`app/data/hr_policy_handbook.md`) returning exact section citations (`Section X.Y`).
2. **WorkWeek MCP Toolset (`https://mock-saas.aishprabhat.demo.altostrat.com/work-week/mcp/`):**
   - Auth: Streamable HTTP with `X-MCP-Token: <WORKWEEK_MCP_TOKEN>` header.
   - Tools:
     - `get_current_employee_id()`
     - `get_employee_balances(employee_id)`
     - `get_personal_info(employee_id)`
     - `get_leave_requests(employee_id)`
     - `request_time_off(employee_id, start_date, end_date, leave_type, days)`
     - `update_personal_info(employee_id, address, phone)`
     - `cancel_leave_request(employee_id, request_id)`
   - Resource Helper Tool: `get_employee_profile(employee_id)` reading MCP resource `workweek://employees/{employee_id}/profile` (returns `employee_id`, `first_name`, `last_name`, `email`, `job_title`, `department`, `role`, `hire_date`, `manager_id`, `home_address`, `phone_number`).
3. **ServiceImmediately MCP Toolset (`https://mock-saas.aishprabhat.demo.altostrat.com/service-immediately/mcp/`):**
   - Auth: Streamable HTTP with `X-MCP-Token: <SERVICEIMMEDIATELY_MCP_TOKEN>` header.
   - Tools:
     - `list_tickets(employee_id)`
     - `create_ticket(requested_by, category, short_description, priority, assignment_group='Service Desk')`
     - `add_ticket_comment(ticket_id, author, comment)`
     - `update_ticket_status(ticket_id, status, resolution_notes='', updated_by='System')`
   - Resource Helper Tool: `get_ticket_details(ticket_id)` reading MCP resource `serviceimmediately://tickets/{ticket_id}` (returns full ticket record and comment activity timeline).

## Constraints & Safety Rules
1. **Strict Tenant Isolation & Anti-IDOR (`before_tool_callback`):**
   - Every tool call accepting `employee_id` or `requested_by` is intercepted before execution and compared against `session.state["verified_employee_id"]` (resolved deterministically from `get_current_employee_id()`). Any mismatch or attempt to query/modify another employee's profile, leave balance, or tickets is deterministically blocked with `ACCESS_DENIED: Cross-tenant/cross-employee access is strictly prohibited`.
2. **Input & Output Guardrails (`SafetyGuardrailPlugin` — ADK `BasePlugin` + Model Armor):**
   - Screens every incoming user prompt and outgoing model response via Google Cloud Model Armor API (when `MODEL_ARMOR_TEMPLATE_ID` is set) and deterministic in-process guardrails:
     - **Prompt Injection & System Leak Prevention:** Blocks jailbreaks (`Ignore previous instructions`, `DAN`, `system prompt`, `reveal tool schemas`, `override employee_id`).
     - **Off-Topic & Community Guidelines Enforcement (Section 5.5 & 9.2):** Refuses political debates, trolling/ad hominem attacks, coding homework, recipes, or non-HR/IT topics with a polite enterprise refusal message.
     - **SPII Inspection & Redaction:** Blocks or masks national IDs (Singapore NRIC/FIN `[STFGM]\d{7}[A-Z]`, US SSN), credit card numbers, and bank account numbers in prompts, logs, and responses.
3. **Deterministic Business Rule Enforcement (`before_tool_callback`):**
   - **WorkWeek Leave Validation (Section 1.2):** `start_date` and `end_date` must be valid `YYYY-MM-DD`, `start_date <= end_date`, `start_date` not in the past, and `days` must not exceed remaining balance.
   - **WorkWeek Contact Validation:** `address` >= 5 characters; `phone` matches `^\+?[\d\s\-()]{7,20}$`.
   - **ServiceImmediately Ticket Lifecycle State Machine (Section 5.5):** Enforces sequential progression `New -> In Progress -> Resolved -> Closed`. Blocks compliance violations that bypass intermediate states (specifically blocking `New -> Closed` directly, even though the raw mock backend allows it, because Section 5.5 of the Policy Handbook states: *"Bypassing intermediate states (e.g., moving a ticket directly from New to Closed) is a compliance violation and is strictly prohibited"*).Closed tickets cannot transition.
   - **ServiceImmediately Priority Inflation Downgrade (Section 5.5):** If `priority='1 - Critical'` is requested without active outage/crash/downtime keywords, or for minor facility issues (e.g., squeaky office chair, cosmetic wear), programmatically downgrades priority to `'4 - Low'` per Section 5.5 (`"Minor facility issues, such as a squeaky office chair or cosmetic wear, must be classified as '4 - Low'"`).

## Success Criteria
1. **Unit & Guardrail Contract Tests (`uv run pytest tests/unit`):** 100% pass rate across all deterministic guardrails (anti-IDOR, prompt injection, off-topic refusal, SPII redaction, `New -> Closed` compliance block, squeaky chair priority downgrade to `4 - Low`, phone/address regex, chronological leave validation).
2. **Integration & MCP Connectivity (`uv run pytest tests/integration`):** Live MCP tool discovery and end-to-end tool execution against `mock-saas.aishprabhat.demo.altostrat.com` using `X-MCP-Token`, plus FastAPI `/chat` and `/healthz` verification.
